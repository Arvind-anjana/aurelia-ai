I. The Classical Proportion (Palladio)

Andrea Palladio’s harmonic scale was rooted in Pythagorean musical intervals—translating string vibrations into room dimensions: 1:1 (unison), 3:2 (diapente), and 4:3 (diatessaron). In Villa Rotonda, spatial perfection is achieved through bilateral symmetry where voids are symmetrically punctuated by load-bearing limestone masses. The envelope acts as an acoustic instrument of stillness.
