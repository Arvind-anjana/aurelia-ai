Project Apollo Mission Summary
The Apollo program was designed to land humans on the Moon and bring them safely back to Earth. 
The first manned mission to the Moon was Apollo 8, which orbited the Moon in 1968. 
Apollo 11 was the spaceflight that first landed humans on the Moon on July 20, 1969. Commander Neil Armstrong and lunar module pilot Buzz Aldrin formed the American crew that landed the Apollo Lunar Module Eagle.
The total cost of the Apollo program was approximately 25.4 billion dollars.

Voyager Interstellar Mission
The Voyager program is an American scientific program that launched two unmanned space missions, Voyager 1 and Voyager 2, in 1977. 
Voyager 1 was launched on September 5, 1977. As of 2023, it is the farthest human-made object from Earth. 
Both spacecraft carry a golden record, which contains sounds and images selected to portray the diversity of life and culture on Earth to any extraterrestrial life form.

Mars Exploration Rovers
The Mars Exploration Rover (MER) mission was a robotic space mission involving two Mars rovers, Spirit and Opportunity. 
Spirit landed on Mars on January 4, 2004, and Opportunity landed three weeks later on January 25.
While they were only designed to last 90 Martian days, both rovers functioned for years beyond their expected lifetime. Opportunity remained active until 2018, when a severe planet-wide dust storm covered its solar panels.
